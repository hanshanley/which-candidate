# V4: bounded new-source acquisition, unchanged statistical design

Frozen before V4 fits. V1-V3 and protected historical outputs remain immutable.
All 449 Democrats, the 405 qualified performance candidates and all fifteen
testing-family blocks remain in scope. This successor does not search model
families, redefine the outcome or weaken numerical/category/geographic gates.

The acquisition journal and registry pin 91 new observations from 11
evidence-yielding originals. The source population is selectively documented
state legislators, questionnaire respondents and profiled candidates, not an
all-candidate census. Preserve every literal fragment, candidate-contest key,
source clock/hash, source population and conflict. The five V3 rejected
cross-contest transfers remain rejected.

## Factual and analytical decisions

- Exact historical birthdays support exact age at November 3, 2026.
  Month/year or year-only information supports an interval; use exact age only
  when its endpoints agree. Separate age-band sensitivity requires the whole
  interval to fit a declared band; no January 1 birthdays or midpoints.
- Explicit dated religious affiliation and ordained clergy descriptions
  support source-recorded religion. Broad Christian remains other Christian;
  upbringing, surnames, relatives and endorsements are not affiliation.
- Explicit completed credentials in pre-election editions, or explicitly
  completed before earlier dated events, support the highest evidenced level.
  Graduation without a stated level, attendance and undated post-election
  completion remain factual-only. New lower credentials do not erase higher
  already-qualified credentials.
- Retain the four existing historical occupational families. A dated prior
  occupation can qualify as experience, not necessarily current employment.
  Multiple supported families remain ambiguous. Clergy, organizer, nonprofit
  executive and engineer do not automatically map into the four codes.
- Office histories in the new batch explicitly mean before the 2026 cutoff,
  not before first House entry. Government means separately recorded
  non-elected governmental roles. Preserve positive histories, but no original
  service-versus-nonservice contrast without supported negative responses.
- Multiyear former military service supports positive pre-election service;
  absent sections, advisory councils, family service and veterans' advocacy
  do not support either positive service or a nonservice reference.
- Reinspection of the held CAWP workbook/codebook adds no observations:
  historical Democratic Asian American/Pacific Islander (6), MENA (2) and
  Native American/Native Alaskan (1) categories fail the ten-person gate.
  Preserve the restricted historical Black-versus-White women comparison.
  Hispanic/Latina is not the complement of any race category. No new all-person
  race model or manufactured non-Hispanic reference is introduced.
- Review already-held FEC records separately from acquisition. Require exact
  full name, state, party, House district and election year, with one FEC ID
  and consistent explicit classification. Two reviewed alias bridges use
  independently acquired official Ford and Harvie biographies. Multiple IDs,
  unbridged aliases and ambiguous contest assignments remain held.
  Election classification does not set verified federal-service flags.

## Analysis and reuse

Use the V3 fifteen-block estimands, lean and lean-squared controls, fixed
fifteen-test Holm family (unavailable p=1), state/person-separated folds,
training-only nuisance transformation, rank/covariance support checks and
complete outer-state-deletion prediction intervals. Keep joint Democratic/
Republican incumbency distinct from its Democratic coefficient.

Reuse a V3 block only when its complete candidate-level numerical inputs,
identities, state/person groups and controls are identical. Rebuild selection
decisions and the full-family correction even for reused blocks. Recompute
affected association, sensitivity, matched-sample predictions and full-population
failure diagnostics. Retain all sparse/unsupported cohorts and distinguish
restricted validation from full-population validity.

Report new observations, first-informing cells/candidates, newly qualified held
facts, analytical eligibility and fitted support separately. All new evidence
is retrospectively retrieved; prospective availability remains unestablished.
Observational associations are not causal nominee-replacement effects, and the
donor association is not a universal moderation claim. Publish local reports,
affected figures and both complete downloads only; no website deployment.
