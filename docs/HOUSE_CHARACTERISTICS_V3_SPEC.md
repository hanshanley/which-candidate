# House characteristics V3: acquisition successor specification

This exploratory specification follows the V1/V2 results. It was written after
the acquisition review and before V3 outcome fits. V1, V2 and all earlier frozen
outputs remain immutable. The baseline is commit 30d8a8c; the research queue
contains all 449 Democrats and fifteen traits, including the 405 qualified
performance comparisons and all 37 unresolved federal-incumbency records.

## Evidence and identities

Use the reviewed incremental-ingestion journal, exact six-field candidate/contest
identities and original-byte receipts. Preserve every historical normalized claim.
Shared documents are acquired once, hashed once and validated using complete
CSV/XLSX rows or reviewed biography fragments. Offline replay may use exactly
matching normalized claims when historical originals are absent. An attempted
search, directory shell, namesake or denied request is not acquired evidence.

The new source-family batch comprises the CAWP 1990-2024 congressional women
candidates workbook and methodological codebook, explicit With Honor service
labels, CFE candidate biographies, and a Minnesota legislative-library biography.
The 2026 CAWP candidate CSV is byte-identical to the held source: its coding is
an existing-evidence qualification, not new acquisition. Track new documents,
claims, candidate-trait cells, newly informed candidates, corroboration and
analytical changes separately, with Democratic and incumbent/challenger totals.

Historical CAWP joins require exact full name, state, party, House office and
district in 2024. Different-district matches remain held without a reviewed
person bridge. Preserve literal codes and overlapping classifications. CAWP
uses self-identification or multiple-source verification: do not label all rows
direct self-identification. Explicit W supports White; H supports Hispanic/Latina,
not a race or a non-Hispanic complement. Unknown never supplies a reference.
The women-candidate classification is the source definition, not inferred sex.

CAWP 2026 I/C/O classifications may fill unresolved election incumbency only
when the exact candidate/contest is matched and no qualified contrary service
record exists. They do not prove lifetime nonservice or supply House tenure.
Keep official service verification distinct from this retrospective control.

Religion preserves literal descriptions, observation/retrieval dates and whether
the source reports or quotes a candidate. Current undated CFE/With Honor pages
contain post-election updates: qualify explicit factual reports separately from
pre-election analytical eligibility. Religious allies are not nonreligious.
Scientist is not a religious affiliation. Blank religion and None Reported
minority classifications remain unknown. Contractor/advisory membership is not
proof of government employment; elected service is a separate trait.

## Unchanged estimands and support

Retain all fifteen blocks: age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion, education,
profession, prior elected office, other government, military, federal incumbency,
House service, donor ideology, congressional voting ideology and party unity.
Keep outcomes, lean/lean-squared controls, candidate identities and contest holds.
Use incumbent-only verified-service cohorts for legislative records. Donation,
voting and campaign ideology are distinct. Missing records are not zero scores.

Use frozen V2 values as the starting point, adding only explicitly reviewed
evidence decisions. New exact birth information can support age at November 3,
2026; retain year-only intervals and the whole-interval age-band sensitivity.
Dated completed credentials and historical occupations can inform retrospective
background analyses; post-election-only current affiliation does not.

An amended race question uses *only explicitly White or Black CAWP 2024 women
candidates* matched to the 2026 inventory. This is a selective historical-source
cohort, not an all-candidate racial effect. Retain all other identities and
overlaps factually; exclude both-Black-and-White records from the two-group
contrast rather than forcing exclusive identity. No fabricated non-Hispanic
reference. Office, government and military positive histories may be qualified
facts without a supported service-versus-nonservice contrast. Evaluate support
before fitting; report unavailable comparisons explicitly.

Preserve V2 minimum counts, geographic support, full-rank and conditioning
checks; clustered association uncertainty; matched-sample state-held-out,
person-purged predictions; training-only preprocessing; common-incumbency-support
validation and complete outer-state-deletion intervals. Do not waive failed
folds or use pseudoinverses/ridge to manufacture intervals. Retain unrestricted
population failures separately from restricted validation claims.

Maintain the fixed fifteen-block Holm testing family, including unavailable
tests at p=1. Report the joint D/R-incumbency block separately from each
coefficient. No causal nominee-replacement or universal moderation interpretation.
Report sample size, state coverage, selective missingness, sparse groups and
coding/timing exclusions for every block. Reuse unchanged statistical helpers
and preserve unaffected historical results; create V3 outputs only.
