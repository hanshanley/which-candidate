# V8: national nonincumbent evidence and supported analysis

Recorded before V8 effects. V1-V7 and all native candidate-contest identities
remain frozen. The population remains 1,284 candidate-contest records, including
449 Democrats and 405 eligible Democratic performance comparisons.

The fifteen trait definitions, outcome, lean/lean-squared and D/R-incumbency
adjustments, fifteen-test Holm family, category minimum ten across three states,
fit minimum thirty across ten states, and full rank/conditioning gates are
unchanged. State-held-out/person-purged prediction and complete outer-deletion
uncertainty retain training-only preprocessing and explicit failed populations.
No new model family or significance-driven specification search is authorized.

## New source families

Official voter pamphlets contain candidate-submitted statements, not independent
verification. Preserve literal field labels, wording, edition, PDF page, original
hash and source subject. Deduplicate identical statements across county editions.
An opponent named in a statement is not its subject. Candidate lists, contents
pages, downloaded navigation shells and search snippets are not biographical
observations.

In a pre-election pamphlet, an explicitly paired academic degree and institution
supports a documented credential; a school alone, attendance or a certificate
does not. This extends the existing completed-credential list convention to
explicit candidate-submitted credential lists, without calling them independently
verified highest attainment. Preserve contradictory or incomplete credentials.

Preserve current occupation separately from the occupational-history field.
Professional coding uses the existing occupational families; mixed families and
undefined job meanings remain held. Do not equate public office with government
employment, advisory service with a paid job, or a military spouse with service.
Office history means elected office before November 3, 2026, not before first
House entry. An explicit "None" under "Elected Experience" can support a scoped
candidate-reported negative after checking all retained positive histories.
"Prior Governmental Experience" is broader and must not automatically become
either no elected office or no paid government employment. Blank, N/A, omitted
sections and missing database records remain unknown. A military negative
requires an explicit answer about the candidate's own military service.

National questionnaires and original campaign, institutional, service and
published-interview biographies must match the native candidate and contest.
Retain publication dates and factual reference dates separately. Retrospective
retrieval is not prospective availability. Birth years/dated ages remain
intervals; no invented birthday. Public self-descriptions may qualify identity
facts but do not enter incompatible historical author-classification contrasts.
Religion requires a dated, named affiliation or self-description under the
existing historical-questionnaire definition, not upbringing or family religion.

## Existing analyses requiring explanation

V7's full-population religion failure is the Iowa fold: IA03 supplies the only
Republican-incumbent opponent in its fitted population. Holding out Iowa leaves
that nuisance coefficient unidentified; the current implementation rejects both
IA02 and IA03 together even though IA02 has a supported nuisance configuration.
V8 will test row-specific estimability of training-only redundant nuisance
columns. It may retain a test row satisfying every training linear relation,
but must withhold every row violating any relation, record its candidate key
and failure reason, and retain the complete target denominator. No coefficient
is imputed for an unsupported row. Category, training-size, rank and conditioning
failures still withhold the entire fold. Complete full-cohort predictions and
outer deletions remain prerequisites for full-population uncertainty.

The historical Latino-indicator question remains author-coded 0/1 among
independently linked historical officeholders. Export state and incumbent/
nonincumbent composition, overlapping samples, exclusions and the already
declared competitive/source/deletion robustness. Do not generalize its
association to all candidates or confuse it with predictive improvement.

supplementary fields, retained-evidence requalification and remaining actionable

Report every trait's denominator, unknown count, factual/analytical coverage,
