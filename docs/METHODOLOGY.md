# Measurement contract

The unit is a candidate in a dated contest, not a voter. Keep House and Senate,
regular and special elections, counting rounds, and reporting vintages separate.
Native district labels are not evidence of stable district boundaries.

The primary eventual outcome is Democratic two-party vote share minus a
cross-fitted constituency/chamber/cycle expectation. A descriptive presidential
benchmark gap is a different quantity, especially in midterms. One share point
equals two D-minus-R margin points. Neither quantity identifies a causal nominee
effect or demographic voting behavior.

The initial importer is deliberately narrower:

- It retains the full selected FEC worksheet queries, including primary-only
  candidates, source accounting rows, missing counts and footnotes.
- Candidate totals add disjoint party lines, never combined totals plus their
  components. Repeated combined totals are reconciliation references.
- Main-sample eligibility requires one identifiable D nominee and one R nominee
  with numeric counts. Unopposed candidates are retained, not assigned an
  invented opponent or a performance score.
- Specials, unresolved rounds, Louisiana's election system, ranked-choice
  contexts and ambiguous accounting remain held pending specific qualification.
  Holdings remain visible in complete outputs rather than being dropped.
- 2024 House counts imported from the participation audit are previous-endpoint
  source facts, not a certified cross-year geographic panel. The audit's 2026
  provider snapshot is provisional, not certified or independently complete.
- Candidate identity inferred within one native contest is not a verified
  cross-election person match.

Later feature analysis must use pre-election evidence. Full-career ideology
scores can leak later votes. Newcomers without federal service have structurally
missing legislative records, not zero ideology. Party unity, bipartisan voting,
issue positions and attendance are separate measures.

Include winners and losers in biography acquisition. Race/ethnicity and religion
require documented public self-identification, never inference from names or
photos. Unknowns, multiple identities and time-varying evidence remain explicit.
Birth in the state is distinct from residence and other local ties.

Model House and Senate separately with chronological and candidate/geography
group-aware validation. Report sparse samples, missingness, uncertainty,
alternative benchmarks and null findings. No characteristic ranking should be
published before the corresponding evidence and validation exist.
