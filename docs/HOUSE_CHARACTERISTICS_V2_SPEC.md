# Evidence-qualified House characteristics v2: exploratory amendment

This amendment follows inspection of v1 and its failed validation ledgers, and
preceded the first v2 fits; the diagnostic amendment below followed the first
build. It is exploratory, not preregistered. Preserve v1 and all
earlier frozen outputs. Retain the same fifteen hypotheses, outcome, lean
controls, primary coefficient scales, support gates and fifteen-test Holm family.
Unavailable comparisons remain in that family with p=1.

## Evidence review and eligibility

Review the held institutional, previous-office, candidate-supplied questionnaire,
official ballot-designation and endorsing-organization batches specified in the
review registry, plus all eleven Democratic official candidate profiles. Seal
each exact candidate/contest/profile hash. Source status neither proves nor
disqualifies a fact. Retain literals, dates, source definitions, qualifications
and conflicts. An endorser's account is attributed to the endorser.

Distinguish factual qualification, standardized coding, pre-election timing,
field-level analytical eligibility and estimable contrast support. Explicit
positive race/ethnicity classifications qualify as observations without supplying
negative/reference identities. Multiple explicitly supported identities remain
multiple. Historical religion is historical; questionnaire refusal is not an
affiliation. Attendance, earned credentials, occupational family, paid employment,
elected office, appointed/government work and military service remain distinct.
Unknown history never becomes nonservice. Previously reviewed Directory facts
remain qualified. Undated changeable claims do not become election-day inputs.

Use the two held exact FEC/person/service crosswalks for Keating and Suozzi in a
v2-only panel overlay. Require matching Bioguide, state/district and a House term
containing Election Day. Do not repair other unknown incumbencies by name absence.
Keep unknown Republican controls and multi-candidate holds.

Birth years supply intervals only. Qualify explicit secondary-source birth-year
reports as such, not as exact birthdays. Primary age remains exact completed years.
Add a separately labelled age-band sensitivity: under 40, 40-59, 60 or older.
An interval contributes only if its entirety lies in one band. Do not impute a
midpoint, invent a birthday or assign a band across a boundary.

Retrospective inputs remain prospectively ineligible. A credential/history is
temporally eligible only with an explicit pre-cutoff publication or claim date,
or an identifiable pre-election candidate-guide context. Profession codes describe
reported occupational families, not verified paid work or exhaustive careers.
Preserve all old normalized observations and original descriptive flags.

## Validation repair and its narrower estimand

The exact-age v1 deletion failures lose the Republican-incumbent nuisance stratum
when Michigan and Virginia are both outside training. Gender loses its sole
nonincumbent, Colin Allred, when Texas is held out. These are identification
failures, not grounds for zero imputation, rank-check removal or a pseudoinverse.

For each block retain full association-cohort prediction diagnostics, including
failed folds. Separately declare the supported predictive population using only
covariates, never outcomes: retain joint D/R-incumbency strata observed in at
least three states. Freeze that population before full-pipeline deletion fits.
This ensures a stratum can survive outer state deletion plus inner state holdout,
but does not waive focal-rank, category or conditioning checks.

Baseline and augmented models train on exactly the same supported population.
All transforms and nuisance-reparameterization decisions use training observations
only. Initially only constant-nuisance removal was specified. The first v2 build
showed that removing the unsupported open-seat stratum made D/R incumbent nuisance
columns complementary in education, leaving its design rank-deficient in every
fold. The diagnostic amendment permits removing a nuisance column only when its
training values are an exact linear combination of retained baseline columns AND
that identical fitted relationship holds for every test observation (absolute
tolerance 1e-10, no relative tolerance). This includes constant columns and
D-incumbent + R-incumbent = 1; it never extrapolates an unsupported test stratum.
Otherwise the fold is unsupported.
Do not drop focal columns. Hold out entire states and purge repeated people.
Save every training/test ID, person, rank, condition, exclusion and failure reason.
Prediction on the restricted population is not full-population validation.

Compute predictive intervals only with every matched prediction and every
delete-one-state full-pipeline prediction complete. Refit after deletion without
reselecting the original predictive population. Incomplete cases remain diagnostic
without intervals. Report full versus restricted denominators side by side.

sensitivities. Compare retained v1 values on their original cohort where
appropriate; the age-band sensitivity has its own sample and is not a second
primary age discovery. Report source selection, sparse categories, unknown
incumbency and outcome exclusions rather than forcing a joint complete-case fit.

collected versus newly qualified existing observations, newly estimable contrasts,
