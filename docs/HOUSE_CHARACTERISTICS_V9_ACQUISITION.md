## Controls, supported analyses and limitations

All three Democratic incumbency holds are resolved. Paired signed Form 2
records establish the Wilson and Khalifa person bridges. Walker's dated
WRKF/WWNO personal answer explicitly denies prior elected office and the
same report plus retained federal service identify Higgins as incumbent.
No nonexistent Walker FEC match is invented. Only Khalifa/SC02 adds an eligible
control-complete contest; Walker and Wilson remain performance-ineligible.

The unchanged specifications require ten candidates in three states per
category, thirty candidates in ten states per fit, fixed fifteen-test Holm
adjustment, training-only processing and state-held-out/person-purged
prediction. Models use share percentage points, not margin points.

The joint D/R-incumbency, selective historical donor-ideology and restricted
historical Latino blocks pass Holm correction. None of the matched predictive
gain intervals excludes zero. The Latino association remains 89 historically
classified officeholders in 27 states: 83 incumbents and six nonincumbents,
with coefficient +4.9324 share pp and pointwise interval [2.8415, 7.0233].
Its prediction-uncertainty population is 87, not all 89 or all 449 Democrats;
the gain interval [-0.4202, 1.1165] does not establish predictive improvement.
Population, state counts, comparison support, overlap, exclusions and existing
sensitivity/deletion checks are in `historical-latino-population.json`.
No representativeness, causality or nominee-replacement effect is claimed.

Religion remains 122/123 full-fit predictions. Holding Iowa out removes the
training support for IA03's opponent-incumbency configuration. This is not a
software failure cured by dropping IA03 or assigning an arbitrary coefficient.
It requires additional compatible training support or a separately specified,
validated extrapolating/regularized model. The present method is preserved.

### Specific remaining prerequisites

- Age: explicit dated age, birth date/year or conservative election-date
  interval; an undated literal age cannot be assigned its retrieval date.
- Gender, race, ethnicity and religion: named explicit public self-description
  or a permitted source-defined classification, with timing and conflicts
  retained. Names, photographs, relatives, geography and party are not evidence.
- Education: attendance versus completed attainment, completion date and
  compatible analytical category. Undated credentials remain factual reports.
- Profession: explicit roles and career dates, multiple careers and compatible
  coding; ballot labels alone do not measure a complete career.
- Prior elected office: explicit scoped negative and positive histories across
  sufficiently supported categories. Broad governmental-experience `None`
  does not automatically mean never elected.
- Other government employment: evidence of paid non-elected employment and
  comparable explicit negatives; advisory, elected, volunteer and contractor
  roles are not automatically paid public employment.
- Military: 37 analytical values are **36 positives and one explicit negative**.
  At least ten comparable negatives across three states are needed before
  the existing contrast can pass category support, in addition to fit gates.
  Directory `None`, omitted service and database absence cannot supply them.
- Incumbency: no Democratic hold remains; other-party held identities stay in
  the all-party queue. New facts still require qualified person bridges.
- House service, voting ideology and party unity: verified dated service
  crosswalks and compatible pre-election voting records. First entrants can
  be structurally inapplicable; missing records are not zero ideology.
- Donor ideology: documented compatible donor-score coverage and identities;
  selective historical populations cannot be extended by imputation.
- Eight source-subject holds: Jen Mazzuckelli's retained biography lacks the
  required literal native-person homepage bridge; seven California printed
  variants lack the stricter legal-name/identity support. These are source
  holds, not eight Democratic incumbency holds or necessarily unknown traits.

These are evidence and methodological prerequisites, not proof that no
additional public source could ever be found. Further acquisition should
start from the exact per-candidate gaps and genuinely new originals, not
repeat completed source families or target desired coefficients.

## Exact reproduction commands

Run from the repository root with its locked environment. The first workflow
is optional live acquisition/raw-cache extraction; it is not required for
normalized offline replay and must respect source access/reuse restrictions:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -m which_candidate.characteristics_v9_acquisition --acquire --audit-cached
.venv/bin/python -m which_candidate.characteristics_v9_extract
.venv/bin/python -m which_candidate.characteristics_v9_review
.venv/bin/python -m which_candidate.characteristics_research --offline
```

The current integrated build retains frozen historical fits, refreshes the
root browser and rebuilds complete downloads:

```bash
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv/bin/python -m which_candidate.house_project --evidence-only
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv/bin/pytest -q
.venv/bin/ruff check which_candidate tests scripts
.venv/bin/ruff format --check which_candidate tests scripts
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv/bin/python scripts/validate-characteristics-v9.py
.venv/bin/python scripts/check-characteristics-v9-browser.py
```

Download and verify the exact release assets, then use a **new, nonexistent**
work directory. The wrapper denies networking and verifies that the guard is
active, rebuilds every archived member and both complete ZIPs byte-for-byte:

```bash
gh release download characteristics-v9 --repo hanshanley/which-candidate \
  --pattern complete-queries.zip --pattern substack-figures.zip --dir downloads
.venv/bin/python scripts/replay-characteristics-v9.py \
  --archive downloads/complete-queries.zip \
  --workdir /tmp/which-candidate-v9-replay
```

The validation receipt records pre-receipt replay. The separate release receipt
records receipt-inclusive replay and actual asset-download byte/SHA verification,
avoiding circular archive hashes. No website deployment is part of reproduction
or delivery.
