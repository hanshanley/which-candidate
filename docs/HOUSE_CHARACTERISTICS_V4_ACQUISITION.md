## Coding and time reference

New service facts are recorded as **history before November 3, 2026**, with
explicit office/service intervals. A history before first House entry is a
different variable and must not silently replace that definition. Multiple
positive roles remain multiple observations. Only explicit negative responses
can establish nonservice. Assess known-positive category comparisons separately;
do not manufacture a reference or relax sample/geographic support.

Source identity, literal supporting fragment, source observation/publication
date, retrieval date/method and hash are mandatory. Unknown source dates remain
unknown. An immutable birth fact or dated completed credential can support a
retrospective analysis; a post-election undated religious or employment statement
does not establish the corresponding pre-election characteristic.
