## Results and limitations

The full specification is `HOUSE_CHARACTERISTICS_V1_SPEC.md`. Ten blocks support
association estimates. Historical donor ideology and joint D/R incumbency clear
the declared fifteen-block Holm correction. The Democratic-incumbent coefficient
alone does not: its estimate is +0.76 share points (95% CI -0.31 to +1.84).
The donor association is +1.77 share points per +0.5 rightward score units
(95% CI +0.81 to +2.73; n=151, 39 states).

Age, gender, religion, education, profession, congressional service, voting
ideology and defection do not clear that correction. The prior-voting association
has the opposite sign to donor ideology, but different constructs and selective
cohorts cannot establish a generic moderation effect. Race, ethnicity, prior
elected office, other government experience and military comparisons are
unavailable, not null findings: qualified contrast/reference evidence is absent.
Those hypotheses remain in the correction family with p=1.

The outcome is the qualified Democratic two-party-share presidential gap,
controlling for district lean and lean squared and known D/R incumbency.
Legislative/service analyses use verified incumbents. Each block has its own
reported sample; baseline and augmented predictions use identical candidates,
holding out states and repeated people together. No completed predictive
comparison has a 95% improvement interval wholly above zero. Incomplete folds
are diagnostic only. Full-pipeline state deletions, competitive-seat and
incumbent-only sensitivities, sparse-category rules and stricter education
coding are downloadable. Missingness is selective. None of these estimates
identifies the causal effect of replacing a nominee.

## Replay and publication

With the pinned environment:

```bash
python -m which_candidate.characteristics_research --offline
python -m which_candidate.house_project --evidence-only
pytest
ruff check .
ruff format --check .
```

The supported incremental ingestion validates exact candidate/contest/name/
state/district/party, fragments, dates, hashes and descriptive status before
merging with `house_candidates.build(inputs=...)`. Historical raw caches are
not required. Both complete downloads contain normalized inputs, receipts,
review data, code, tests, reports and all derived tables. The figures ZIP embeds
the exact complete-query ZIP. See `DOWNLOADS.md`. No website deployment.
