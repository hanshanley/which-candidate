This amendment is recorded before V7 effect estimates are computed. V1-V6,
historical normalized observations, native candidate-contest identities, and
the five V3 rejected cross-contest transfers remain protected.

## Population and unchanged design

Retain all 1,284 candidate-contest records and all fifteen traits. The Democratic
research population is 449, with 405 eligible performance comparisons. V6 has
197 exact-age, 296 gender, 54 restricted-race, 170 religion, 206 education,
100 profession, 438 incumbency and 23 positive military analytical values.
Positive values do not establish an estimable service-versus-nonservice contrast.

The fifteen-test Holm family, unavailable tests at p=1, category minimum ten
across three states, fit minimum thirty across ten states, design/covariance
checks and state-held-out/person-purged validation are unchanged. Preprocessing
is training-only. Report full-population failures separately from supported

search model families or relax support rules in response to estimates.

## New sources and restricted classification questions

Lublin's House election-winner table covers 2012-2020; its 33-state legislative
winner table covers 2011-2020. These are historical source-classified winners,
not universal 2026 self-identification or a representative challenger sample.
Read both archive codebooks and retain every original indicator and missing code.
The author's `race` variable includes Latino and overlapping classifications;
the separate `ethnicity` column often records detailed Asian/Pacific origins.
It must not be interpreted as universal Hispanic/non-Hispanic identity.

For an explicitly labelled exploratory historical-classification comparison,
an actual source `latino=1` versus `latino=0` is permissible. Blank indicators
remain unknown; a White or Black race label alone never supplies a zero.
This is the author's Latino indicator, not proof of present non-Hispanic
self-identification. Require concordance across matched records and preserve
conflicts and overlapping classifications.

For race, assess historical author-classified Black versus White records,
excluding mixed/overlapping classifications from that binary comparison.
The source classifies Middle Eastern origin as White; disclose this definition.
If combining with existing CAWP observations, require concordant definitions
and no known mixed/conflicting category. Explicitly label the expanded
historical-officeholder source population rather than retaining a misleading
women-only caption. Unsupported or conflicting identities remain factual
observations without an analytical binary value.

House linkage requires an independently qualified native FEC/Bioguide link,
matching full recorded name or explicit alias, state, and a dated House term
corresponding to the source election. State-office linkage requires full name,
state and independently recorded legislative service, with a retained literal
linkage fragment/source. Name/state matches alone remain discovery leads.
Historical state districts and parties never overwrite native contest keys.

## Timing and remaining traits

Office history means elected office before November 3, 2026, not before first
House entry. Elected-winner tables supply positive histories, never nonservice.
Government employment requires an explicit job/appointment; an advisory title
alone is insufficient. Military omissions and blank questionnaire/database
fields remain unknown. No positive-only list completes these contrasts.

The newly acquired CongressData FST has 1,229 columns, unlike the retained
1,045-column deposit. Its actual `MILITARY` label "No military service
mentioned" is an omission category, not an explicit negative. Do not promote
the corresponding rank zero or a collapsed zero to verified nonservice merely
because a shorter codebook description says "no military service." Positive
historical service remains usable when independently linked. Similarly,
Hunt's pre-Congress political-experience indicators are not office-before-2026
negatives, and degree-or-equivalent indicators do not identify specific
completed postgraduate credential levels. Retain them without forcing the
existing education, profession or office contrasts.

Birth years and dated reported ages supply defensible election-age intervals,
not invented birthdays. Exact-age estimation requires a singleton age.
Completed credentials and dated occupational meanings follow the existing
education/profession rules, including mixed-career holds. Dated historical
church membership is recorded as such, never silently relabelled current
2026 affiliation; unavailable or conflicting current timing remains explicit.
Source-recorded identity can qualify factually without an estimable contrast.

The incumbency review permits orthographic accent normalization while retaining
full tokens and order, and equivalent House at-large 00/AL notation. It does
not invent nickname, middle-name, inverted-name or duplicate-ID bridges.
An independent literal bridge must be retained for those cases. FEC election
classification is not verified lifetime federal nonservice.
