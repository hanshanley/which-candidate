## Weaknesses that remain, rather than being "fixed"

Donor samples are selective, especially for challengers and Senate contests.
Scores describe historical coalitions, not 2026 platforms. Calendar-valid
pre-election captures do not guarantee source authorship or correct machine
subject attribution. Shor's January 2025 release ends in 2022 and C3 covers
2018 state-legislative candidates; neither is a complete 2026 candidate census.

Lean controls and state-cluster/delete-one-state errors do not remove opponent
quality, candidate selection, benchmark noise or all omitted constituency
factors. New tests are exploratory, with pointwise rather than multiplicity-
adjusted intervals. Original six-trait Holm results remain separate.
Historical plots do not test causal changes or replicate Split Ticket WAR.

Counts remain provisional. Missing religion/race data cannot be filled from
names, photos, relatives or constituency composition. No claim that these
traits "do not matter" is warranted.

## Visual review and reusable practice

The old figures were not repaired by palette changes alone. The replacement
uses one question per chart, plain-language labels, explicit denominators,
aligned small multiples, restrained serif/paper/terracotta/blue styling,
interpretable uncertainty and full-size CSV/caption companions.

Actual individual PNGs were reviewed, including article-width and reduced
previews. Automated checks cover clipping, dimensions, reproducible bytes,
background-only theme conversion, sparse-fit display and missing-roster
labels. These support integrity; aesthetic judgment is not an objective test
or proof of substantive truth.

The publication-charts skill is tracked and installed byte-for-byte in the
personal skill directory. CLI executable-based discovery was not tested
because the executable was unavailable.
