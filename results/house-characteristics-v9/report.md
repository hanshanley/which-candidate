This local successor preserves V1-V8 and all 1,284 native candidate-contest records. The Democratic denominator remains 449, with 405 performance-eligible comparisons. No website is deployed. The fifteen traits are retained whether estimable or not.

Previously reported but unqualified fields, supplementary citations and identity-only filings are not counted as first evidence.

Two signed-filing reviews and one dated questionnaire resolve 3 Democratic incumbency controls; 0 remain held. 1 performance-eligible contests gain complete controls. These changes are separate from newly measured background traits.

## What the analyses support

The outcome is Democratic two-party House share minus the map-qualified presidential Democratic share, in share percentage points, not margin points. Models retain lean, lean squared and the specified D/R incumbency controls. The fifteen-test Holm family includes unavailable blocks at p=1. Categories need ten candidates in three states; fits need thirty candidates in ten states. No coefficient-driven acquisition or significance-seeking model search was introduced.

Association estimates are observational and source-selected, not the effect of replacing a nominee. New descriptive identity facts do not extend the restricted historical author-coded contrasts. Unchanged numerical signatures are reused; changed populations are recomputed under the same specifications.

## Controls, structural eligibility and prediction failures

For Antonio Wilson/LA05 and Zyon Khalifa/SC02, both signed FEC statements share the filed legal name, principal campaign committee, filing address and signing date. Both IDs remain in the review. Their retained explicit election classifications agree. This is not a classification based merely on matching names or agreeing flags, and does not independently verify lifetime nonservice. Only Khalifa was performance-eligible. Caleb Walker/LA03's dated personal answer denies previous elected office, while the report and retained service crosswalk positively identify Higgins as incumbent. This resolves Walker's election nonincumbency without creating an FEC match. The exact ballot nickname also bridges his retained Louisiana filing. Walker and Wilson are performance-ineligible; only Khalifa adds a complete-control contest.

The lagged voting measure is 118th-Congress Nokken-Poole ideology, not current campaign ideology. A unique verified crosswalk plus the Clerk's explicit zero-prior-Congresses and 119th-Congress swearing-in establishes structural inapplicability for qualifying first entrants. Missing rows alone do not. Other unknown or unresolved service populations stay explicit; neither ideology nor party unity is filled with zero.

Religion retains the V8 row-specific training-estimability rule. IA03 is the only Republican-incumbent-opponent configuration in the fitted religion population; holding out Iowa leaves that nuisance coefficient unsupported. IA02 is supported. IA03 is not silently dropped or assigned an extrapolated coefficient, and complete full-population uncertainty is not claimed.

## Unsupported comparisons and remaining prerequisites

Office, paid-government-employment and military contrasts still require comparable explicit evidence on both sides under the existing definitions and support thresholds. The single retained military negative is not a veteran and is insufficient. No prior elected office, no government employment and no military service are different claims. Blanks, N/A, omitted service, private corporate boards, relatives' careers and absence from databases supply no negative category.

Remaining factual gaps need named, explicit original evidence; analytical gaps may instead need credential completion, career meaning, pre-election timing or source-compatible comparison support. Future Caucus's aggregate methods mix concrete dates with graduation-based age estimates; no compatible candidate-level microdata was acquired or inferred. Unresolved aliases require stronger native-person bridges, not relaxed name matching. The complete per-candidate queues identify present statuses, not proof that no additional public evidence exists.
