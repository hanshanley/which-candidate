## Literal review and exclusions

Race is a restricted historical source-classified Black/White comparison, expanded beyond CAWP women only through independently linked historical officeholders. Mixed, overlapping and discordant classifications are held. Lublin classifies Middle Eastern origin as White; incompatible explicit identity definitions are not silently pooled. This is not universal current racial self-identification.

One previously analytical mixed Black/Asian record moves to a hold under the preregistered unmixed definition. Its qualified facts remain retained; 183 newly eligible Democratic fields are a net increase of 182 after this hold.

The ethnicity block asks a restricted historical author-coded Latino-indicator question. Actual source zeros, not race labels or missing rows, supply its reference. Detailed Asian/Pacific origins remain separate. Blanks, conflicting records and discordant explicit Hispanic/Latina descriptions remain held. Historical winner samples are selective and incumbent-heavy; newly available estimation does not make them representative of all 449 Democrats.

Gronli's completed BS is explicit in the dated university interview. Her reported age 53 on May 1, 2026 establishes an election-age interval 53–54, not an invented birthday. USDA state-director employment is explicit; design/marketing is not forced into a business-owner category. The 2011 Nevada biography documents church membership only at that historical date, not verified 2026 affiliation.

Accent-only full-token normalization qualifies the unique held Dueñas FEC classification. The other actual hold causes are retained individually in the acquisition log. Missing middle-name/nickname bridges and multiple candidate IDs remain unresolved; none are chosen merely because statuses agree. No election-ineligible record is promoted to outcome eligibility.

Office histories mean before November 3, 2026, not before first House entry. Positive elected-winner lists do not establish a no-office group or exhaustive mutually exclusive career histories. Actual military omissions remain unknown. Office, other-government and military contrasts remain unavailable without defensible negative references. All earlier evidence and five rejected cross-contest transfers remain protected.
