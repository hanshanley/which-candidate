# Candidate backgrounds: national evidence, selective inference

## What this release establishes

All 1,284 native candidate-contest records and all fifteen traits remain visible, including 449 Democrats and 405 eligible Democratic performance comparisons. V1–V7 remain frozen. This is a local research product; no website was deployed.

New qualified first evidence fills **65 candidate–trait fields for 43 candidates** across parties. A first-evidence field must previously have been unknown and now have qualified factual evidence. Downloaded pages, rejected proxies, supplements and identity-only observations are not interchangeable with those gains.

Retained FEC evidence qualifies 7 Democratic incumbency controls; 3 remain held. 1 eligible contests gain both controls. Control unlocks are not new background facts.

The national affiliation table explicitly reports a November 16, 2026 update. Its new named reports are factual post-election observations, not pre-election model values. Navy biographies and the targeted VOTE411 page could not be acquired; snippets supplied no registered claims.

Candidate-submitted pamphlets preserve exact question labels and wording; they are not independently verified biographies. Completed dated credentials are distinct from attendance, in-progress degrees and unlevelled 'law degree' or 'graduate' wording. Mixed professions remain held.

The filed Bradstock committee statement explicitly connects the legal candidate to the native candidate's campaign website. Four explicit Washington Elected Experience negatives are scoped no-prior-elected-office claims. Blank, N/A, party/student positions, military-school attendance, spouses' service and church volunteering supply no invented negative, employment, veteran or affiliation values. A later Tennessee questionnaire does supply one explicit no-military-background response; this is not enough for the unchanged ten-candidate/three-state category gate. Office/government/military contrasts remain unavailable; one blocked trait did not halt other acquisition.

## Associations are not candidate effects

Outcome: Democratic two-party House share minus the map-qualified presidential Democratic share, in share percentage points, not margin points. Existing models adjust for constituency lean, lean squared and the specified incumbency controls. The fifteen-test Holm family retains unavailable blocks at p=1. Categories require ten candidates in three states; fits require thirty candidates in ten states. No new model family or significance-seeking contrast was introduced.

Three blocks clear Holm correction: joint D/R incumbency, historical donor ideology and the restricted historical Latino indicator. The Democratic-incumbent coefficient alone does not establish an advantage. Higher donor CFscore is associated with better performance in its selective donor-linked sample, not proof that changing a nominee's ideology changes votes. No matched predictive-gain interval excludes zero.

## Religion: corrected software, retained failure

On the frozen V7 religion association population, training-only nuisance estimability corrects 121/123 full-fit predictions to 122/123. The Iowa-2 row is estimable from training data; Iowa-3 has an unseen opponent-incumbency stratum and stays unsupported. No test outcome determines the reduction, no unknown control is imputed and the 123-candidate target is not silently narrowed. Current-release counts appear above; full-population fold files retain exact row failures. Earlier fits remain immutable.

## Restricted historical Latino indicator

The frozen V7 association covers 89 candidates in 27 states: 83 incumbents and only six nonincumbents, with no unresolved incumbency. It does not represent all nominees. Its +4.932 share-pp adjusted association (95% interval 2.841–7.023; Holm p≈0.000701) uses explicit historical author-coded 0/1 values and independently linked people. Zeros are not inferred from White labels or missing records. The restricted paired prediction population is 87; its MAE-gain interval [−0.420, 1.117] includes zero. Association evidence is not predictive improvement or a causal nomination effect.

`historical-latino-population.json` exports every included candidate, state counts, incumbent/challenger composition, overlap with all other fields, exclusion counts and the already specified competitive/source sensitivities. The preserved state-deletion files provide existing influence checks; no new model search was run.

## Remaining limitations by trait

- **Age:** Exact age remains incumbent-heavy; year/month intervals are separate.
- **Source-recorded gender:** Explicit official filing labels retained factually; incompatible source definitions not pooled.
- **Race:** New filing classifications are not silently pooled into the historical author Black/White estimand.
- **Ethnicity:** Existing restricted historical author-coded Latino indicator only; no universal current contrast.
- **Dated religion:** Explicit dated descriptions only; new challenger coverage remains selective.
- **Education:** Completed supported level only; attendance, undated timing and contradictions held.
- **Professional family:** Four historical families only; mixed families and outside occupations held.
- **Prior elected office:** Four scoped guide negatives; insufficient comparison support; undated histories separate.
- **Other government:** Explicit jobs distinguished from advisory, elected and contractor roles; negative reference absent.
- **Military service:** Dated positive service and one explicit scoped no-military-background response; the negative category remains below ten candidates in three states.
- **Federal incumbency:** Unique legal-name/authorized-campaign/website bridges only; ambiguous IDs stay held.
- **House service:** Verified current federal incumbents only.
- **Historical donor ideology:** Selective donor-linked population, not universal moderation.
- **Prior voting ideology:** Lagged Congress-specific incumbent record; not campaign ideology.
- **Party defection:** Cutoff-qualified current incumbent record; newcomers are not zero.
