## Literal review and exclusions

Biss's August 1977 birth month fixes election age without inventing a day. Boafo and Pearson have explicit birthdays; Benitez-Thompson retains a year-only 47–48 interval. Completed BA/BS, master's and doctorate statements qualify degrees; Pearson's Bowdoin listing alone does not establish graduation. Biss's dated mathematics teaching supports the historical education profession. Oracle government affairs is not automatically business management; licensed social work alone is not proof of clinical healthcare practice.

Pearson's explicit Liberation Christianity description is factual at retrieval but remains analytically unavailable: the undated profile even mentions the future 115th General Assembly. A requested historical-session URL is not an archived historical document. No affiliation is inferred from upbringing or Black/Latino/Jewish caucus membership.

FEC legal-name pages explicitly authorize Lee McInnis, Ray Pooley, Jake Johnson, Tom Perriello and Ace Parsi campaign-name committees in the matching 2026 House seat and party. These links support Challenger election classifications, not independently verified federal service. Jake Johnson's latest filing is after election day; this reconstruction is retrospective, never a prospective predictor. Surname-only Williams and Bradstock committees do not establish the needed aliases. Multiple FEC IDs and other unresolved identities remain held.

Office histories mean before November 3, 2026, not before first House entry. Positive office categories and overlaps are exported separately; missing categories do not imply exclusive histories. No explicit negative office, government or military reference was acquired, so the original contrasts remain unavailable. Race remains the restricted historical CAWP Black/White women comparison; it is not generalized to all candidates. All five rejected cross-contest transfers and all earlier evidence remain protected.
